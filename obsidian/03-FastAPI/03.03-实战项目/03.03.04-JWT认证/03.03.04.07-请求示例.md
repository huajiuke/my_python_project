# 请求示例

> 上级：[[03-FastAPI/03.03-实战项目/03.03.04-JWT认证/03.03.04-JWT认证|03.03.04-JWT认证]]

```text
注册
POST http://127.0.0.1:8080/users/register
Content-Type: application/json

{"username": "authuser", "password": "abc123", "age": 20, "email": "authuser@example.com"}

登录
POST http://127.0.0.1:8080/auth/login
Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded

username=authuser&password=abc123

访问当前用户
GET http://127.0.0.1:8080/users/me
Authorization: Bearer <access_token>
```
